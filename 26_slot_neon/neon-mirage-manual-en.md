# Neon Mirage — How to Play

Neon Mirage is a neon-lit, 5-reel video slot from the Velvet Strip Casino, playable entirely in the browser. There's nothing to install, and credits have no real-world value — it's a themed toy built around chasing wilds across nine lines at once.

## Opening the game

Open the HTML file (or the published page) in any modern browser, desktop or phone. You'll see the glowing cabinet with its flicker-on marquee, a five-by-three grid of reels behind glass, and a control deck underneath. On a phone, a "Machine / Pay Table" switch appears above the cabinet — more on that below.

## Spinning

Click the **SPIN** button on the control deck, or press **Space** or **Enter** on a keyboard. Each spin costs your total bet in credits, deducted the moment the reels start turning. All five reels spin at once and stop in a staggered cascade, left to right.

## Reading a result

Neon Mirage checks **nine fixed paylines** at once, not just the middle row — straight lines across the top, middle, and bottom rows, plus six zigzag and V-shaped patterns that weave between rows. Every payline is diagrammed in the **Pay Table** tab so you can see exactly which cells each one reads.

A payline pays when three, four, or five matching symbols land consecutively starting from the **leftmost reel** — a match that breaks partway across (say, in reel 3) doesn't continue past the break. The **WILD** symbol (a pink-to-cyan starburst) substitutes for any other symbol in a run.

When a spin wins, the matching cells light up with a gold ring, the result strip glows gold with your total winnings, and it lists how many lines paid out. A losing spin turns the result strip a soft pink and reads "No win — try again."

## Betting

Your wager is set **per line**, not as one lump sum. The −/+ stepper adjusts your **line bet** between 1 and 20 credits; since all nine lines are always active, your **total bet** (shown in the other readout) is always nine times your line bet. The **MAX** button jumps your line bet to the highest value you can currently afford across all nine lines.

## The pay table

Open the **Pay Table** tab (or, on wide screens, just look to the right of the cabinet) to see every symbol's payout for 3-of-a-kind, 4-of-a-kind, and 5-of-a-kind, plus a diagram of every payline and an explanation of how WILD works. Rarer symbols pay more:

| Symbol | ×3 | ×4 | ×5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cherry | 2 | 5 | 15 |
| Lemon | 3 | 8 | 20 |
| Bell | 5 | 12 | 30 |
| Horseshoe | 8 | 20 | 50 |
| Diamond | 12 | 30 | 80 |
| Bar | 20 | 50 | 150 |
| Seven | 40 | 100 | 400 |

Every multiplier above is applied to your **line bet**, not your total bet — so a 3-cherry line at a line bet of 2 pays 4 credits, credited once per winning line. Multiple lines can win on the same spin, and their payouts all add together.

## Running low on credits

If your credits drop below what nine lines at the minimum bet would cost, a **REFILL** button appears on the control deck. Tapping it tops your balance back up to 500 credits at no cost — there's no real money involved anywhere in this game.

## Sound

A "sound: on/off" toggle sits at the bottom of the control deck. It mutes the reel-stop ticks and the win chime; your last choice is remembered.

## On a phone

Below 780px wide, the pay table moves to its own page. Two tabs — **Machine** and **Pay Table** — appear above the cabinet so each screen gets full width instead of being squeezed side by side. On wider screens (tablets and desktops) both panels simply sit next to each other and the tabs disappear.

## Saving your progress

Your credit balance, line bet, and sound setting are saved in that specific browser on that specific device (using local browser storage), so they'll still be there next time you open the same page there. They don't sync across different browsers or devices, and clearing that browser's site data will reset them back to the starting 500 credits.

## One more thing

Neon Mirage has no real money value and isn't gambling — it's a themed toy. Play for fun, and if the reels ever run cold, the REFILL button is always right there.
