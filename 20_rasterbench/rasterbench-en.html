Everything runs locally. Images are converted entirely within this page. When you save a file, your browser or app shows a confirmation for each one. "Download all" walks through the converted files one at a time, asking you to confirm each save.
Converting to JPEG or GIF discards transparency — the background is filled with white. GIF output is reduced to a maximum of 256 colors and saved as a single frame (if you load an animated GIF, only the first frame is used).
