Geospatial · DEM to mesh

GeoTIFF (DEM) → OBJ / STL Converter

Converts GeoTIFF elevation data — SRTM and similar sources, .tif / .tiff — into a terrain mesh (OBJ or STL) entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. The converted file loads directly into PolyBench 3D Converter and similar tools.

GeoTIFF files are parsed with geotiff.js, loaded from a CDN (an internet connection is required). GeoTIFFs recorded in latitude/longitude (EPSG:4326, degrees) are projected to real-world meters using a simple equirectangular approximation. GeoTIFFs already in a projected coordinate system are used in their native linear units.
